Buffett Investment Memo — Costco (COST)

Recommendation: OWN

Costco demonstrates strong financial performance and consistent profitability. Its return on equity remained above 15% throughout the five-year period, ranging from 25.1% to 31.2%.

Owner earnings increased from $3,501 million in FY2022 to $9,382 million in FY2026, indicating stronger cash generation over time. Owner earnings were positive in all five years.

Costco passes all three Buffett financial checks, suggesting it is a high-quality business with consistent profitability and growing cash generation.

Decision: I trust the Buffett-style assessment of Costco's business quality because the recommendation is supported by financial calculations from SEC EDGAR. However, the analysis does not consider stock valuation, so it does not establish whether Costco is attractively priced.
