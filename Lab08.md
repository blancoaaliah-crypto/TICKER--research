Lab 08 — Ulta Beauty Peer Comparison
Define / Discover

Target company: Ulta Beauty, Inc. (ULTA)

Comparison date: September 1, 2026

Question: What would Ulta Beauty's stock be worth using P/E multiples from similar companies, and how does that compare with my DCF valuation?

Ulta makes money by selling beauty and wellness products in stores and online. Since Ulta has positive annual earnings, I can use P/E as another way to estimate its value.

I need to find public companies that are similar to Ulta, along with their annual diluted EPS and stock prices on the same date.

Peer Policy

A peer should:

Sell beauty, personal care, hair care, cosmetics, or similar products.
Sell products directly to customers.
Have positive annual diluted EPS.
Have a stock price available on September 1, 2026.

I would still use a company if it has some differences from Ulta, as long as the overall business is similar enough.

I would exclude companies that mainly make money from unrelated products or manufacturing.

Peer Candidate 1 — Sally Beauty Holdings (SBH)

Decision: Qualify

Why it belongs

Sally Beauty sells beauty products such as hair care, hair color, styling products, and professional beauty supplies.

It is similar to Ulta because both companies sell beauty products directly to customers.

Important difference

Sally Beauty focuses more on hair products and professional beauty supplies, while Ulta sells a wider mix of cosmetics, skincare, fragrance, hair care, and other beauty products.

Fiscal year-end: September 30, 2025

Annual reported diluted EPS: $1.89

September 1, 2026 closing price: $16.37

P/E Calculation

$16.37 ÷ $1.89 = 8.66×

Peer Candidate 2 — Bath & Body Works (BBWI)

Decision: Qualify

Why it belongs

Bath & Body Works sells personal care, fragrance, and body-care products directly to customers through stores and online.

It is similar to Ulta because both operate in the beauty and personal-care retail industry.

Important difference

Bath & Body Works mainly sells its own branded products, while Ulta sells products from many different beauty brands.

Fiscal year: 2025

Validation

$16.37 ÷ $1.89 = 8.66×

This matched my calculator.

If I remove Bath & Body Works, the peer valuation changes from a range of $153.43–$222.08 to only the Sally Beauty estimate of $222.08 per share.

The estimate goes up because Bath & Body Works has the lower P/E. Removing it also means I have less information to compare with Ulta.

The peer valuation is much lower than my DCF valuation.

The DCF looks at Ulta's expected future cash flows, while the peer P/E method looks at how similar companies are valued by the market.

The difference does not automatically mean one method is wrong. The peer companies are not exactly the same as Ulta.

Question That Could Change My Decision

How much higher should Ulta be valued if it has stronger growth, margins, and a broader product mix than these peers?

Conclusion

Decision: Watch / Defer

The peer P/E method gives a value of about $153.43–$222.08 per share, while my DCF gives a value of about $449.85 per share.

The peer companies are not perfect matches for Ulta, so I would not use the peer range by itself to decide Ulta's value.

However, the large difference between the peer valuation and my DCF makes me want to be more careful with my DCF assumptions.

I would want to see stronger evidence that Ulta's growth and margins support the higher DCF value before changing my decision.

Things that could change my decision include:

Changes in Ulta's sales growth.
Changes in profit margins.
Updated company guidance.
Changes in my DCF assumptions.
Finding a company that is even more similar to Ulta.
