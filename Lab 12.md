6. Sensitivity vs. Uncertainty

Sensitivity shows how much my results change when I change an assumption.

It does not tell me how likely that assumption is.

For example, the 6% revenue growth case produces a higher value, but my sensitivity analysis does not prove that 6% growth will actually happen.

Because of this, I would want to research Ulta's future revenue growth expectations and company guidance before becoming more confident in my forecast.
